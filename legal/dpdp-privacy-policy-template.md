# ParkIndia — Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Policy

**Last Updated:** [Date]

This Privacy Policy explains how ParkIndia ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal data in compliance with the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA)** of India.

## 1. Data We Collect (Data Minimization)
We only collect personal data that is strictly necessary to provide our parking finder and booking services:
- **Vehicle Information:** Vehicle Registration Number (License Plate) for booking validation.
- **Location Data:** GPS location (if permitted by you) to show nearby parking availability.
- **Booking Details:** Check-in and check-out times, and selected parking lot.

*Note: We do not collect or store financial/payment information. Payments are handled externally if applicable, though the current app focuses on finding parking space.*

## 2. Purpose of Processing
Your data is processed exclusively for:
- Facilitating real-time parking spot reservations.
- Validating entry and exit of vehicles at parking facilities.
- Displaying distance and directions to parking lots.

## 3. Data Residency and Storage
- All personal data is hosted on servers physically located within **India**.
- We do not transfer your personal data across borders.

## 4. Your Rights under DPDPA
Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:
- **Access:** Request a summary of personal data being processed.
- **Correction:** Request correction or updating of inaccurate data.
- **Erasure:** Request deletion of your personal data when it is no longer required for the purpose it was collected.
- **Grievance Redressal:** Register a complaint regarding data processing.

## 5. Data Security
We implement robust, industry-standard security measures, including data encryption in transit and at rest, to prevent a personal data breach.
